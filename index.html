05 — HELP BUILD WHAT'S NEXT
AN INVITATION TO FUTURE PARTNERS

BACK THE
NEXT WAVE.

UNMUTED is an independent Australian-first AI music initiative. We've built this early website to show the vision. Now we're looking for organisations and supporters who may be interested in helping us develop a working platform for music discovery, community participation and future charts.

LOOKING FOR A HOSTING PARTNER

Our current website is a working concept, hosted on GitHub Pages. To grow UNMUTED into a real community platform, we'll eventually need hosting and technical infrastructure for artist profiles, music submissions, user accounts, comments and chart voting.

WHAT WE'RE SEEKING

A conversation about sponsored or discounted Australian hosting, scalable infrastructure, or technical guidance as the project develops.

WHAT A PARTNER COULD RECEIVE

Recognition as a founding technology supporter, a website acknowledgement and opportunities to be part of the platform's development—subject to an agreed partnership.

No fixed infrastructure specification or sponsorship package has been agreed. We'd welcome a practical discussion about what's feasible.

We're in the prototype stage. Sponsorship and partnership opportunities are open for discussion; no partnership is implied or announced here.

07 — OUR DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
BUILDING IT ONE STAGE AT A TIME

THE ROAD
AHEAD.

UNMUTED is starting with a working concept, not pretending to be a finished music platform. Here's the direction we're working towards — with each stage dependent on resources, technical support and community growth.

01 / NOW

THE PROTOTYPE

A public concept website, playable music showcase, interactive chart demonstration and the beginnings of an Australian AI music community.

IN PROGRESS
02 / NEXT

THE PLATFORM

Secure hosting, artist profiles, track submissions, listener accounts and community comments — developed and tested in manageable steps.

PLANNED
03 / FUTURE

THE TOP 40

Fair voting controls, chart rules, verified rankings and a regularly updated Australian AI music Top 40 shaped by its community.

LONG-TERM GOAL

This roadmap is a statement of intent, not a delivery schedule. Features and timing may change as the project develops.
